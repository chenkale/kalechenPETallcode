The spatial resolution is determined by reconstructing a vertically held rod source at various points in the FOV. Only events in the photopeak are used, and an average of 7e5 events are obtained per position. We reconstruct with OSEM (CASToR) with 1 iteration and 16 subsets with (1mm/0.5mm) voxels with no smoothing. We follow NEMA style fitting to determine the FWHM and FWTM by interpolating surrounding bin heights and to determine the position of the peak with parabolic fit of the highest three bins. To account for the width of the source, we subtract in quadrature the active width of the source (1.4mm). The resolution is reported over (a 1cm z region at the center of the FOV/the entire z FOV)
